/ Your questions

The answers, simply.

Another question? The assistant at the bottom of the page answers immediately, or message us on WhatsApp.

I don’t know what to write. Is that a problem?+

Not at all. That is exactly our job: we ask the questions, we write, you correct.

Can you write in other languages?+

Yes, in English and French. For other languages we work with native proofreaders; we can discuss it on our first call.

Do I need to provide professional photos?+

No. Images are included in the price: we choose visuals that suit your business. If you have your own photos, even taken on a phone, we make them shine.

Can I change the copy later?+

Yes. As long as we work together, just send us your changes.
