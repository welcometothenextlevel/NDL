Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1.Data controller

Next Digital Level, Amin Hamdi
475 Spencer St, West Melbourne VIC 3003, Australia
Email: contact@nextdigitalevel.com
Phone: +61 488 894 068

2.The data we collect

  • When you fill in a form on Facebook or Instagram: your name, your phone number, your email address (optional), the suburb or town your business is in and your answers to the form questions.
  • When you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email or through this site: the information you send us.
  • When you visit this site: technical data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) processed by our host to keep the site running and secure.

3.Why we use your data

We use your data only to:

  • call you back and answer your enquiry;
  • prepare a website proposal and arrange a meeting;
  • issue a quote and an invoice, and carry out the work;
  • meet our legal obligations.

We never sell your data and we do not use it for any other purpose.

4.Sharing your data

Your data is shared only with the providers our business needs: Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram forms), GitHub (site hosting, typefaces included), along with our communication tools (phone, WhatsApp, email).

In the “Our work” section, a preview of a client’s site can be shown in an embedded frame. That preview loads only if you click “Live preview” yourself; the site in question then receives your IP address, exactly as if you had opened it directly.

Some of these providers may process data outside Australia, in particular in the United States. In that case we take reasonable steps, such as the providers’ contractual commitments, to make sure your information is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

5.How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that go no further: your data is deleted at the latest 12 months after our last exchange.
  • Clients: your data is kept for the duration of the engagement, and accounting records for 5 years, as Australian tax law requires.

6.Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, you may at any time request access to your data or its correction, ask us to delete it or withdraw your consent. Simply write to contact@nextdigitalevel.com. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au

7.Cookies

This site uses neither advertising cookies nor tracking tools. No cookie is set, and no visitor statistics are collected.

Your browser keeps only two technical preferences, stored on your device and never transmitted: the light or dark theme and, for the duration of your visit, your conversation with the assistant. That assistant runs entirely in your browser: your messages are sent to no server.

8.Changes

We may update this policy. The date shown at the top of this page is that of the latest version.
